# Walkthrough - Luxury Streetwear flagship loader

We have implemented the ultimate luxury streetwear loading animation matching the aesthetic of Zara × Represent × Fear of God × Corteiz.

## Luxury Streetwear flagship design details

1. **Background:** Soft warm-white/cream background (`#F8F7F4`).
2. **Badge:** Central matte black rubber brand logo badge with simulated light sweep (diagonal shimmer line) reflections.
3. **Progress Indicators:** A micro-thin black progress loading bar underneath.
4. **Typography:** Spaced, uppercase monospaced text `"CRAFTED FOR THE BOLD"`.
5. **Transitions:** Smooth fade + scale exit/entry animation wrapped inside `AnimatePresence`.
